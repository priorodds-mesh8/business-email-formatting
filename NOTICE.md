# Sources and scope

This package contains original instructions and fictional examples. It does not include private correspondence, the ASD-STE100 standard, its dictionary, or another author's skill text.

The research drew on these public references:

- [LibreFang email-writer](https://github.com/librefang/librefang-registry/blob/main/skills/email-writer/SKILL.md): business email structure.
- [Anthropic internal-comms](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/internal-comms/SKILL.md): selection of formats by message type.
- [Microsoft accessible email guidance](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accessibility/outlook/make-your-outlook-email-accessible-to-people-with-disabilities): lists, headings, spacing, and accessible links.
- [Government of Canada accessible email guidance](https://a11y.canada.ca/en/making-accessible-emails/): semantic structure and ordered steps.

Paragraph lengths and list sizes are adjustable defaults. They are not experimentally proven limits. The package makes no response-rate claim or accessibility certification claim.

STE100 and anti-slop are optional companions. They are not required for installation or use. This project has no affiliation with ASD, OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, Microsoft, or Vercel.
